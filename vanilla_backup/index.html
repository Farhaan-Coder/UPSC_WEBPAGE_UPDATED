GS II • Polity
YOU
Why is India called a 'quasi-federal' state? Explain with examples for a Mains answer.
ZONE AI

India combines a federal division of powers with a strong Union. For a balanced answer, organise the idea into three parts:

01
Federal foundations

A written Constitution, division of powers and an independent judiciary.

02
A strong Union

Emergency provisions and a single citizenship create a unitary tilt.

03
Cooperative practice

The GST Council shows the Union and states working together.

Read alongside: NCERT • Indian Constitution at Work; Article 246.
Ask a follow-up or try "Give me a 150-word structure"
AI is a study aid, not an authority. Verify important facts against original sources.
